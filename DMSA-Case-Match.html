Cheat sheet
  • Normal · Even cortical uptake with no focal defect.
  • Scar · Persistent low uptake on follow-up, often with cortical thinning or an indented contour.
  • Acute · Recent febrile UTI with reduced uptake, usually with preserved contour or swelling.
